OpenMarket

A desktop point-of-sale app for small shops: ring up sales by barcode, keep stock in check and see your takings, all from one local database.

Latest release: v1.14.0 · Windows · macOS · Linux

Download View source

Features

  • Sell by barcodeScan or type a barcode to add a product to the order. Weighted products ask for the quantity before they go on the ticket.
  • Thermal receiptsPrint the order ticket on an Epson-compatible ESC/POS thermal printer, and print product lists straight from the catalog.
  • Products & stockCreate and edit products with price, stock and minimum stock. Enable or disable products, search the list and filter it to low-stock items.
  • Low-stock alertsWhen a sale takes a product below its minimum stock, OpenMarket tells you right away.
  • CategoriesOrganise the catalog into categories, each with its own picture.
  • Photos fetched for youEmpty product photos are filled from Open Food Facts by barcode, and category pictures from Wikimedia Commons.
  • DashboardSee your most-sold products and a takings chart over the last 7 days, a month, 3 or 6 months, or a year.
  • Order historyBrowse past orders by date range and open any order to see its lines.
  • Local & privateEverything lives in one embedded SQLite file on your computer. No server, no account.
